    📊 Import CSV
    Upload a CSV with columns: name, company, email, url, industry, phone, address (only url is required)
    How it works:
    1. Scout searches Google Places for businesses with outdated websites (8 industries × 4 keywords = 32 searches)
    2. Each found business gets its website audited (mobile, performance, SEO scores)
    3. Leads scored 0-100 by likelihood to pay (industry, site quality, business signals, size)
    4. Sorted into 🔥 Hot (70+), 🟡 Warm (45-69), ❄️ Cold (0-44) segments
    5. Cold leads grouped by industry × region × size for targeted campaigns
    6. Hot leads auto-contacted via Brevo email (if enabled)

    ⚠️ Requires Google Places API key — set in Cloudflare Pages env vars or paste in Settings.
