Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Web Gabochie ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit web.gabochie.com and use our services. We believe in transparency and minimal data collection.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and understand how you interact with it.

How We Use Cookies

We use a minimal number of cookies, limited to what is strictly necessary for the site to function:

What We Don't Use

We do not use:

  • Advertising cookies — No ad tracking, retargeting, or behavioral advertising.
  • Analytics cookies from third parties — We do not embed Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar tracking tools that set cookies.
  • Social media cookies — No social media share buttons that track you across sites.
  • Third-party embedded cookies — No third-party scripts that set non-essential cookies.

Client Portal

When you log into the client portal, we use a session identifier stored in your browser's localStorage (not a cookie) to keep you authenticated. This data:

  • Is stored only on your device.
  • Expires after 24 hours.
  • Is cleared when you sign out.
  • Contains only your company key and login timestamp — no passwords are stored client-side.

WhatsApp and Email Links

When you click a WhatsApp or email link on our site, you are redirected to WhatsApp or your email client. WhatsApp and your email provider may set their own cookies. We have no control over their cookie practices.

Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Cookies

Since we only use essential cookies, disabling them may affect basic site functionality (e.g., popup behavior, form state).

Do Not Track

We respect the "Do Not Track" (DNT) browser setting. Our site does not track users across websites, so DNT signals have no functional impact on our site — but we honor the intent.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if our practices change. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We will not materially increase cookie usage without informing you.
