Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a redesign take?+

Most projects are completed in 6-9 weeks depending on the tier and complexity. Launch Pages finish in 1 week, Starter projects in 6 weeks, Growth in 7-8, and Scale in 8-12 weeks. You'll have full visibility through your client portal throughout the process.

Do I need to provide content?+

For Growth and Scale tiers, we handle content strategy, copywriting, and optimization as part of the package. Starter tier clients provide their own content, but we offer add-on content services if needed.

What if I don't like the design?+

Every tier includes revision rounds — 2 for Starter, 4 for Growth, unlimited for Scale. You'll also preview your redesign in your client portal before launch. Our process ensures we're aligned before any code is written.

Will my SEO rankings be affected?+

We perform a full SEO migration on every project — preserving your existing rankings and implementing improvements. Most clients see improved rankings within 60-90 days after launch due to better performance and structure.

How is this different from Wix?+

Wix gives you a template. We build custom, hand-coded websites that you own 100%. No platform lock-in, no recurring fees, faster performance, better SEO, and conversion optimization built into every page.
