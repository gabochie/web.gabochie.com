Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a redesign take?+

Most projects are completed in 6-9 weeks depending on the tier and complexity. Essentials projects typically finish in 6 weeks, Professional in 7-8, and Enterprise in 8-12 weeks. You'll have full visibility through your client portal throughout the process.

Do I need to provide content?+

For Professional and Enterprise tiers, we handle content strategy, copywriting, and optimization as part of the package. Essentials tier clients provide their own content, but we offer add-on content services if needed.

What if I don't like the design?+

Every tier includes revision rounds — 2 for Essentials, 4 for Professional, unlimited for Enterprise. You'll also preview your redesign in your client portal before launch. Our process ensures we're aligned before any code is written.

Will my SEO rankings be affected?+

We perform a full SEO migration on every project — preserving your existing rankings and implementing improvements. Most clients see improved rankings within 60-90 days after launch due to better performance and structure.

How is this different from Wix?+

Wix gives you a template. We build custom, hand-coded websites that you own 100%. No platform lock-in, no recurring fees, faster performance, better SEO, and conversion optimization built into every page.
