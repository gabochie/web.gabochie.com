Our Philosophy

Market-Based Management

Developed by Charles Koch, MBM is the unique management framework that guides how we create value, empower employees, and drive innovation across all our enterprises.

Vision

Understanding where and how the greatest long-term value can be created, then aligning resources to achieve that vision.

Virtue & Talents

Attracting and developing people of integrity who have the skills and desire to contribute their best work.

Knowledge Processes

Systematically discovering, creating, and applying the best available knowledge to make superior decisions.

Decision Rights

Ensuring the right people are empowered to make the right decisions, with clear accountability for results.

Incentives

Aligning compensation and recognition with the long-term value people create for the organization.

Continuous Improvement

Applying rigorous self-assessment, benchmarking, and experimentation to drive ongoing transformation.

"Market-Based Management is not a set of business tactics. It is a framework for human flourishing — enabling people to create value by discovering and applying better ways to help others improve their lives."
— Charles Koch, Chairman & CEO
