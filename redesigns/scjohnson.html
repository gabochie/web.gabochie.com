Family Legacy

Five generations of the Johnson family have led with purpose, integrity, and a long-term view.

1886

Samuel Curtis Johnson parquet flooring business founded in Racine, Wisconsin.

1914

First branded product — Johnson’s Prepared Wax — revolutionizes home care.

1955

Raid® introduced, changing pest control forever.

1998

Glade® becomes the #1 air-care brand in the U.S.

2000s

Greenlist™ program launched; commitment to transparency and ingredient safety.

Today

5th-generation family leadership — still privately held, still based in Racine, WI.
