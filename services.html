Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the audit?+

You get a full website score (0-100) across design, mobile, and performance categories. Plus a detailed issues list showing exactly what's broken and what's working. It's free and takes 60 seconds.

How long does a redesign take?+

Starter packages finish in 6-7 weeks, Growth in 7-8, and Enterprise in 8-12 weeks. Timelines vary based on complexity and revision cycles.

Can I pay monthly instead of upfront?+

Yes. Starter: $1,000 + $175/mo. Growth: $2,000 + $350/mo. Enterprise: $3,000 + $600/mo. All 12-month terms. No interest.

Do I own the final website?+

100%. You own everything — the code, the design, the content. No platform lock-in, no recurring license fees. We hand over full ownership at launch.

What if I need a custom feature not listed?+

Enterprise tier covers custom development. For anything beyond, we'll quote it as an add-on. We're transparent about pricing — no surprises.

Will my rankings drop?+

No. We perform full SEO migration on every project. Most clients see improved rankings within 60-90 days due to better performance, structure, and mobile optimization.
